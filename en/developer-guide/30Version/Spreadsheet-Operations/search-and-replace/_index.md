Text operations are complex processes for Excel files, and many factors contribute to this complexity and should be considered during processing. Aspose.Cells Cloud provides a reliable way to search for and replace text across a variety of spreadsheet formats, simplifying the task of locating specific strings and updating them across multiple sheets through a unified **search and replace** operation that works with all formats supported by Aspose.Cells Cloud, such as **XLS, XLSX, XLSM, XLSB, ODS, CSV**, and others. Search and replace lets you locate specific strings in a workbook or a particular worksheet and substitute them with new values, so you can quickly clean up data, correct repeated typos, or apply bulk naming conventions throughout an entire workbook.
